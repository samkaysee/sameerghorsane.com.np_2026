About Bikram Sambat

The Bikram Sambat calendar is Nepal's official calendar, named after the legendary king Vikramaditya. It runs approximately 56 years and 8.5 months ahead of the Gregorian calendar. The BS year begins with Baisakh (mid-April) and has 12 months ranging from 29 to 32 days. Saturday is the official day off in Nepal.
